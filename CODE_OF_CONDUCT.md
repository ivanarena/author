# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in Author welcoming and free from
harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that supports a healthy community include:

- being respectful, patient, and constructive;
- giving and accepting technical feedback in good faith;
- focusing disagreement on ideas and observable behavior;
- respecting privacy and avoiding disclosure of personal or sensitive data; and
- taking responsibility, apologizing, and correcting harm when mistakes occur.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized
attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, publishing private
information without permission, or retaliation against a good-faith report.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
release and support channels, and public spaces where someone is representing
the Author project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the
contact channel listed on the
[repository owner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/ivanarena). Do not put
sensitive report details in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities should use
the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will review reports as promptly and impartially as practical. They
may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily limit participation,
or permanently exclude a participant when behavior threatens a safe and
productive project. Report details will be shared only as needed to investigate
and respond.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards and should recuse themselves when they have a conflict of interest.
